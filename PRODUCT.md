# Product

## Register
Brand. This is a personal portfolio: the design is the product. A visitor's impression is the deliverable.

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers evaluating Sounic Akkaraju for software engineering and AI/ML internships starting September 2026. They arrive from a resume or LinkedIn, decide within a minute whether to keep reading, and want proof of range (ML research plus shipped software) without marketing fog.

## Purpose
Show real, specific work (healthcare ML, NLP, distributed data, forecasting, quant, a shipped Flutter app) and the person behind it, in a form that itself demonstrates front-end craft. The theme switcher is part of the argument: the same content rendered as two committed visual systems.

## Personality
Opinionated, mechanical, loud. Swiss industrial print crossed with punk zine: hard edges, black hairlines, one structural accent colour that can be swapped live, all-caps display type, monospace telemetry. Copy is first person and specific; no buzzwords.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages on cream backgrounds; editorial-magazine serif restraint; glassmorphism as decoration; anything that could be mistaken for a template.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast on every palette (verified token pairs), visible focus rings, 44px touch targets, keyboard-operable everything including the project modal (focus trap, Escape), and a reduced-motion path for every animation.
