# Product

## Register
Brand. This is a personal portfolio: the design is the product. A visitor's impression is the deliverable.

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers evaluating Sounic Akkaraju for software engineering and AI/ML internships starting September 2026. They arrive from a resume or LinkedIn, decide within a minute whether to keep reading, and want proof of range (ML research plus shipped software) without marketing fog.

## Purpose
Show real, specific work (healthcare ML, NLP, distributed data, forecasting, quant, a shipped Flutter app) and the person behind it, in a form that itself demonstrates front-end craft. The theme switcher is part of the argument: the same content rendered as three committed visual systems.

## Personality
Three committed systems behind one switcher. Original: the dark, glassy first design. Neo-Brutalist: Swiss industrial print crossed with punk zine, one swappable accent, hard edges. Contributions: GitHub's dark contribution graph, where Sounic's real last-year graph spells SOUNIC and then becomes a neural network as you scroll. Copy is first person and specific; no buzzwords.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages on cream backgrounds; editorial-magazine serif restraint; glassmorphism as decoration; anything that could be mistaken for a template.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast on every token pair (listed in each theme's stylesheet), visible focus rings, 44px touch targets, keyboard-operable everything including the hero stages (Graph/MLP/CNN buttons), the Neo-Brutalist project modal (focus trap, Escape), the Contributions project cards (buttons that open the tray) and the convolution printer (kernel buttons, slider, arrow keys), and a reduced-motion path for every animation (no pins, no auto-rotation, still frames).
